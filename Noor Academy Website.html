FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer a free demo class?

Yes. Every new student can attend one demo class in any subject before enrolling.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the program and number of subjects. Send us a message and we will share the fee structure on WhatsApp. Sibling and early-payment discounts are available.

Are there separate batches for girls?

Yes, we run separate boys’ and girls’ batches with a safe, supervised environment.

How do parents track progress?

Parents receive a monthly report with attendance, test scores and teacher remarks, and can meet teachers on set days.
